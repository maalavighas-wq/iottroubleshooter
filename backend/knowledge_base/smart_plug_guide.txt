SMART PLUG OFFLINE / NOT RESPONDING

Symptom: Smart plug is shown as offline in the app, or the connected appliance does not turn on/off via the app.

Step 1 – Check physical power:
Ensure the smart plug is firmly inserted into the wall socket. Check that the socket is powered by testing it with another device (e.g., a lamp or phone charger).

Step 2 – Check the plug's LED indicator:
Most smart plugs have an LED indicator. A steady amber or red light usually means it is not connected to Wi-Fi. A steady blue or green light means it is connected and working. Refer to your plug's manual for indicator meanings.

Step 3 – Power cycle the smart plug:
Unplug the smart plug from the wall, wait 15 seconds, then plug it back in. Allow up to 60 seconds for it to reconnect to your network.

Step 4 – Check your router and internet connection:
Verify that your internet is working by opening a browser on another device. Restart your router if needed.

Step 5 – Check Wi-Fi band compatibility:
Smart plugs typically only support 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Ensure your phone is connected to the 2.4 GHz network before adding or re-adding the plug.

Step 6 – Re-pair the plug in the app:
Remove the plug from the app, then add it again using the manufacturer's app (e.g., TP-Link Kasa, Meross, Amazon Alexa, Google Home).

SMART PLUG SETUP / PAIRING FAILURE

Symptom: App cannot discover or connect to the smart plug during setup.

Step 1 – Reset the smart plug:
Hold the physical button on the plug for 5–10 seconds until the LED flashes rapidly. This puts it into pairing mode.

Step 2 – Check your phone's Wi-Fi:
Connect your phone to the 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network you want to use for the plug.

Step 3 – Grant location and local network permissions:
Allow the app to access your location and local network so it can discover nearby devices.

Step 4 – Temporarily disable VPN on your phone:
VPNs can block device discovery on the local network. Disable any active VPN before pairing.

---

SMART PLUG CONFIGURATION ISSUES

Symptom: Schedule, automation, or energy monitoring features not working.

Step 1 – Check app permissions:
Ensure the app has necessary permissions (notifications, background refresh) to run automations reliably.

Step 2 – Verify device time zone:
In the app settings, verify the device's time zone is set correctly for schedules to trigger at the right time.

Step 3 – Update the app:
Check the App Store or Google Play for updates to the manufacturer's app.

---

SAFETY NOTE:
Smart plugs have a maximum load rating (typically 10–16 A, 2300–3680 W). Never exceed this rating. Do not use smart plugs with high-power appliances such as ovens, tumble dryers, or electric heaters unless the plug is specifically rated for them. Unplug the smart plug before inspecting it.
