SMART SPEAKER OFFLINE / NOT RESPONDING

Symptom: Smart speaker (e.g., Amazon Echo, Google Nest, Apple HomePod) is unresponsive or shows offline.

Step 1 – Check power:
Ensure the speaker is plugged in and its power indicator is on. Try a different power outlet.

Step 2 – Power cycle the speaker:
Unplug the speaker's power cable, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. Wait for the startup chime or light indicator showing it is ready.

Step 3 – Check Wi-Fi connectivity:
Verify that other devices on your network are working. Smart speakers require a stable 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection (check your model's specifications).

Step 4 – Check the companion app:
Open the manufacturer's app (Amazon Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home). Check the device status. An "offline" status may resolve on its own after a network check.

Step 5 – Re-connect to Wi-Fi:
In the manufacturer's app, go to the speaker's settings and update the Wi-Fi network. Follow the in-app steps to reconnect the speaker to your network.

---

SMART SPEAKER SETUP / PAIRING FAILURE

Step 1 – Download the official app:
Setup must be completed through the official manufacturer app (e.g., Amazon Alexa, Google Home).

Step 2 – Enable Bluetooth and location services on your phone:
These are required for the app to discover the speaker during initial setup.

Step 3 – Put the speaker in setup mode:
Most speakers enter setup mode when powered on for the first time. For subsequent setups, use the factory reset procedure in the manual (often holding the mute button for 15+ seconds).

Step 4 – Follow in-app setup instructions:
The app will guide you through connecting the speaker to your Wi-Fi network. Ensure you are connecting to a compatible band.

---

SMART SPEAKER AUDIO ISSUES

Symptom: Poor audio quality, echo, or distortion.

Step 1 – Check volume and equalizer settings:
Use the companion app to adjust volume and audio settings.

Step 2 – Check placement:
Avoid placing speakers in enclosed spaces, against walls, or near reflective surfaces. Place the speaker on a flat, stable surface in an open area.

Step 3 – Reduce interference:
Keep the speaker away from other electronics that may cause electromagnetic interference.

---

SAFETY NOTE:
Do not use smart speakers in bathrooms, near water, or in wet conditions. Do not attempt to open the speaker casing.
