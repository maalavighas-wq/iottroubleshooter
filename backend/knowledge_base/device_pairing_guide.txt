GENERAL IoT DEVICE PAIRING GUIDE

This guide covers the common steps for pairing any new smart home device to your network.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN:
- Ensure your Wi-Fi router is working and broadcasting a 2.4 GHz network.
- Have your Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and password ready.
- Charge or plug in your smart device before starting.
- Download the official manufacturer app (not a third-party app).
- Make sure your phone is connected to the same 2.4 GHz network you want to use.

STEP-BY-STEP PAIRING PROCESS:

Step 1 – Download the official app:
Search the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) for the official app. Examples: TP-Link Kasa, Philips Hue, Amazon Alexa, Google Home, LIFX.

Step 2 – Create or sign into your account:
Most manufacturer apps require an account to manage devices.

Step 3 – Grant required permissions:
When prompted, allow the app to access: Location, Local Network, Bluetooth (if required), Notifications.

Step 4 – Put the device into pairing mode:
Power on the device. Many devices enter pairing mode automatically when first powered on. If setting up after a reset, the device may need to be factory reset first (see individual device guides).

Step 5 – Follow in-app discovery:
Tap "Add Device" or the "+" button in the app. Select your device type and follow the on-screen instructions.

Step 6 – Enter Wi-Fi credentials:
When prompted, enter your Wi-Fi network name and password (these are sent directly to the device; they are not stored by this application).

Step 7 – Wait for connection:
The app will communicate with the device and confirm successful pairing. This typically takes 30–90 seconds.

Step 8 – Assign a name and room:
Once paired, give the device a memorable name (e.g., "Living Room Lamp") and assign it to a room in the app.

---

COMMON PAIRING FAILURE CAUSES AND FIXES:

- Wrong Wi-Fi band: The most common cause. Ensure phone is on 2.4 GHz.
- VPN active on phone: Disable VPN before pairing.
- Missing app permissions: Grant Location and Local Network access.
- Device not in pairing mode: Factory reset the device and try again.
- Wrong app: Use only the official manufacturer's app.
- Router with AP isolation enabled: Disable AP (Access Point) isolation in router settings.
- Too far from router: Move the device and phone closer to the router during setup.

---

FACTORY RESET METHODS (COMMON):
- Rapid power cycle (bulbs, some plugs): Turn off and on 5 times quickly.
- Hold button (plugs, cameras, speakers): Hold the reset button 5–10 seconds until LED flashes.
- App reset (if accessible): Use the device settings menu in the app to factory reset.

Note: Factory reset erases all device settings. You will need to re-pair the device and re-configure automations/schedules.
